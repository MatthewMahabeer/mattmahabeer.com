About

I'm Matthew Mahabeer, a software developer with a BSc in Computer Science from The University of the West Indies. In my day-to-day work I build software in the insurance and banking industries, where systems have to be accurate, secure, and dependable.

Alongside that, I take on independent automation projects for small businesses.

What I do

I help small and low-tech businesses replace manual, repetitive work with simple, reliable automation, so customers get faster answers and staff spend less time on admin.

  • WhatsApp ordering and intake: customers place orders, book, or send details through WhatsApp, with a person able to step in at any time.
  • Back-office workflows: orders, receipts, and records flowing into the tools a business already uses.
  • Practical AI: assistants that draft, sort, and summarise, with human review where it matters.
